# Botanical plate prompts: The Operator's Curriculum (akitsuu edition)

For Nano Banana Pro or Nano Banana 2 via Higgsfield. There are 17 plates: one for each skill page (C1 to C7, B1 to B9) and one for the skill tree page.

**How to use**
- Paste the **shared style block**, then the plate's **subject** line.
- Generate at **16:9 landscape**. Each skill page has a slot about 176 mm wide by 62 mm tall, and the plate is scaled to fit it.
- Name each file by its code (`C1.png`, `B4.png`, `TREE.png`) and send them back. I drop them into `curriculum/plates/` and rebuild.

**What to check before you send a plate back**
- **No text.** If the model adds letters or numbers, regenerate. The PDF typesets every label.
- **One red element.** Exactly one, and it is the element the subject line names.
- **No India or MENA motifs.** Use temperate, Japanese-garden or European herbarium species only. This is an akitsuu brand rule.

---

## Shared style block (paste first, every time)

> Antique scientific botanical plate, in the manner of an 18th century herbarium engraving. Fine black-brown ink linework (#2B2321) with delicate stippling and cross-hatching for form. Flat, unshaded warm off-white paper ground (#F5F0E8), with no paper texture, stains or vignette. One single element is filled with a flat crimson red (#C41E3A); everything else is ink line only, with optional sparse flat accents in deep brown (#7D3D1E). Thin straight leader lines point to parts but end in small empty circles. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no signature, no border, no frame. Specimen centred on the page with generous empty margins. No gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D rendering, no photorealism. Calm, precise, museum quality. 16:9 landscape.

**C1 · Financial literacy**
> Subject: a complete flowering plant, from root to fruit, drawn as one specimen. Broad leaves at the top, a strong central stem, a dense root ball. Exactly one ripe fruit hangs from the stem, filled crimson red. To one side, a separate small study of a single water droplet entering the root tips.

**C2 · Unit economics**
> Subject: a seed pod split open lengthwise, showing a row of seeds of very different sizes inside. One seed is far larger than all the others and crowds them; that oversized seed is filled crimson red. A second, smaller study beside it shows the same pod closed.

**C3 · Pricing and value**
> Subject: a stone fruit (a plum) cut in half, cross-section facing the viewer, showing three clear layers: thin outer skin, thick flesh, hard central stone. The middle layer, the flesh band, is filled crimson red. Beside it, a whole uncut plum on a short twig with two leaves.

**C4 · Cash and collections**
> Subject: a potted seedling beside a glass laboratory vessel of water, with a thin tube carrying water from the vessel to the soil. Fine ink marks on the vessel show the water level. One water droplet, falling at the moment it enters the soil, is filled crimson red.

**C5 · Sales and negotiation**
> Subject: two climbing vines of different species growing up a single wooden stake from opposite sides. Their tendrils meet and wind together in one short middle section. That intertwined middle section is filled crimson red; everything above and below is line only.

**C6 · Contracts, tax and compliance**
> Subject: a sequence of four studies of the same seed pod, left to right: tightly closed, beginning to swell, splitting at the seam, fully open. In the final open pod, the released seed is filled crimson red. The earlier pods are line only.

**C7 · Decision quality**
> Subject: a single branch drawn as a pruning study. Several side shoots have clean pruning cuts (drawn as small flat ovals). Others continue to grow. At the main fork, one large bud is filled crimson red. A faint dotted line marks where the gardener would cut next.

**B1 · Client business read**
> Subject: a longitudinal cut through a plant stem showing the vascular channels running upward like parallel tubes. Most channels are open and even. One channel narrows sharply to a pinch point; the narrowed section is filled crimson red.

**B2 · Research**
> Subject: a plant drawn in two halves divided by a soil line. Above ground, a small, tidy plant with three leaves. Below ground, a root system three times larger and far more tangled than the top suggests. One deep root tip is filled crimson red.

**B3 · Insight**
> Subject: a germination sequence of five stages, left to right: dry seed, swollen seed, seed coat cracking, first shoot pushing out, small seedling with two leaves. The crack in the seed coat at stage three, where the shoot breaks through, is filled crimson red.

**B4 · Positioning**
> Subject: a meadow survey plate seen from slightly above. On the left, a dense cluster of near-identical small daisies crowded together. On the right, open empty ground with a single different flower standing alone, its bloom filled crimson red.

**B5 · Brand platform**
> Subject: a single plant shown in vertical layers like a specimen sheet: root system at the bottom, then stem, leaves, and a single flower at the top. The root system at the base is filled crimson red. Leaves and flower are line only.

**B6 · Architecture and naming**
> Subject: three specimens side by side. Left: one stem carrying many identical blooms. Centre: a main stem with three different shoots grafted onto it, the graft union on the main stem filled crimson red. Right: three separate unrelated plants in three small pots.

**B7 · The creative brief**
> Subject: a single flower cut in half to show its anatomy. Many stamens around the edge lean inward and converge on one central pistil. The central pistil is filled crimson red. The petals are drawn open and spread wide around it.

**B8 · Growth and effectiveness**
> Subject: two growth studies side by side. Left: an annual flower drawn three times in a row, blooming, then wilting, then blooming again. Right: a cross-section of a tree trunk showing many concentric growth rings. The outermost growth ring is filled crimson red.

**B9 · Case-study writing**
> Subject: the full life cycle of one plant arranged in a gentle rising arc, left to right: seed, sprout, young plant, flowering plant, plant bearing fruit, fallen fruit with a new seed. The ripe fruit at the peak of the arc is filled crimson red.
