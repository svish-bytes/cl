# Elswheir Design Blueprint: The Operator's Curriculum (PDF)

**Pipeline:** B (modular, repeatable, typography-first), applied to a long-form A4 printed workbook.

**Spatial strategy.** A4 portrait with 18 mm margins and a 12-column grid. Every module page uses the same structure: a large Garamond module numeral and title band at the top, a one-line "why this matters to you" pivot, then two stacked panels. The **Memorise** panel is solid ink-bordered with numbered key points. The **Apply** panel holds exercises tied to Svish's real clients, each with an output and a time box, and ends with a "Done when" line. Lots of negative space between panels lets each short point breathe, following the staccato rhythm.

**Texture matrix.** Warm uncoated stock (#F1ECE2) with a fine fractal-noise paper grain on every page (SVG feTurbulence, multiply, about 9% opacity). There are no smooth gradients anywhere. Accent fields use a risograph halftone dot screen (4 px pitch). Three riso ink colours, one per track: Riso Red (#D5442E) for Business Acumen, Riso Blue (#2D5BA0) for Brand Strategy, and Riso Ochre (#B8862A) for Creative Direction. Spot colour sits flat on the page, the way a two-drum riso print would look. Body text is near-black ink (#1B1A17).

**Typographic system.** Montserrat carries all structure: body at 9.4 pt, labels at 700 weight uppercase with +14% tracking, titles at 800. EB Garamond italic is used only for power words and the large module numerals, creating the modern-vs-classic tension. Line-height is 1.5 for body and 1.15 for display.

**Integration.** The stacked logo ("Els" in Montserrat 800 over "wheir" in Montserrat 200 italic, w aligned under E, very tight leading) sits on the cover and in every page footer. No URL appears anywhere.
